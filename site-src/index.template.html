How it works

Your job, your language, three steps

Qonverse doesn't run any AI itself. You use the AI you already like to write a pack for your exact work, and Qonverse turns it into lessons with sound.

1

Copy our prompt

Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI chat. It asks about your job, the language and the moments you face at work.

2

Get your pack

Short phrases with transliteration and meaning, an example sentence, a mini conversation and the replies you're likely to hear.

3

Listen, imitate, practise

Paste it into Qonverse. Play it normal or slow, loop it, repeat it, then practise in a chat with a guest.

The pack prompt is open source too. Copy it, paste it into your AI, and you'll have your first pack in minutes.

Get Qonverse

Android app or web app — your choice

Same packs, same lessons, and backups move between them. The Android app updates itself; the web app is always the latest version.

Android

Version __VERSION__ preview · Android 8.0 or newer · __SIZE__

Download APK
  1. Open the downloaded file on your phone.
  2. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Open Qonverse — the sample packs are already there.
  4. It checks for updates each time it opens and asks before installing.
  5. Add the “Phrase of the day” widget and a daily reminder if you like.

SHA-256 __SHA__

Web app for iPhone & iPad

Runs in Safari, Chrome or any modern browser. Works offline after the first visit.

Launch web app
  1. Open the web app in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Qonverse from your home screen like any app.
  4. Need a voice? Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.

This is a preview release

  • Qonverse is new and still being tested, so you may find bugs or changes between versions.
  • The sample packs and AI-made packs haven't been checked by native speakers. Have someone fluent review phrases before relying on them with real guests.
  • Voices come from your phone or browser, so pronunciation and the voices available vary by device.
  • The Android preview is installed outside Google Play. Your packs and progress are kept only on your device — use Settings › Backup & restore before changing phones.
  • Provided as-is under the Apache License 2.0. Found a problem? Tell us on GitHub.
